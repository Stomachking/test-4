What each thing on this page is called in Adobe Analytics, and where the number comes from. Channels come from Marketing Channel processing rules, checked top to bottom; the first match wins. Paid / Owned / Earned is not built in: add it as a classification on the Marketing Channel dimension.

Paid

Paid search Paid Search

A click on a search ad: search-engine referrer plus your paid parameter, such as ?cid=ps_brand. Put paid rules above Natural Search and Social Networks.

Programmatic Display

A click on a banner bought through a DSP, matched by a tracking-code prefix such as ?cid=dsp_retarget.

Owned

Email Email

Matched by a tracking code in the link, such as ?cid=em_oct_launch.

Direct Direct

No referrer: a typed URL or a bookmark. Leave "Override last-touch channel" off for Direct.

Earned

Organic search Natural Search

A search-engine referrer with no paid tracking code.

Social Social Networks

A referring domain on the list of social sites.

Referral Referring Domains

Any other outside site. A catch-all rule near the bottom of the list.

Before the visit

Adobe Analytics only sees people once they land on the site. These numbers come from the platforms that sent them, joined on campaign or tracking code.

Emails sent, opened
From your email platform, such as Adobe Journey Optimizer, Adobe Campaign or another email service.
Ad views
Impressions from Google Ads, your DSP or Adobe Advertising.
Search views
Impressions in Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools.
People reached
Reach from each social platform's insights.

Inside the site

Visits
Adobe's word for sessions. A visit ends after 30 minutes of inactivity.
Left after one page
The Bounces and Bounce Rate metrics.
Signed in
Usually a custom success event fired on login, or an eVar that stores logged-in status.
Article reads
Page Views of each article in the Page dimension, or a custom "Content name" eVar with an "Article read" event.
Pages viewed
Page Views ÷ Visits. Adobe also has a Page Depth dimension.
Converted
Any success event you define, such as Orders or a lead-form event. Conversion rate is a calculated metric, such as Orders ÷ Visits.

Audience and geography

Country
The built-in Countries dimension, from the visitor's IP address.
Region
Adobe's Regions dimension means states and provinces. For APAC / EMEA / LATAM, add a classification on Countries.
Device
The Mobile Device Type dimension. Desktops usually show as "Other".
Internal users
IP exclusions remove them from reports entirely. To compare, flag them with an eVar (corporate IP range or login domain) and a segment instead.
Age group
Not collected by Adobe. It comes from an eVar set at login, a Customer Attributes import, or an audience platform.
